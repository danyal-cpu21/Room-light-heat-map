Room Light Heat Map

Mark your furniture, see which spots are too dark or too bright for how you use them, then get priced ideas with the exact spot for each light.

1Set up your room

Photo mode lays a flat top-down map over a perspective photo, so pins can look like they sit on furniture. Use the furniture buttons to mark your bed, desk and so on where they appear in the photo. Lights will then keep off them. Treat spots as approximate.

2What do you want?

Ask the lighting advisor

Tell it what you want in your own words: a mood, a budget, a problem to fix, or something to add (for example “put a reading chair in the top-right corner”). It updates the room and answers from your priced options.
Answers are generated by Claude from your room data and the options in step 3. Prices are estimates.

Add your own idea

Have a specific lamp, bulb or spot in mind? Enter it here with your own brightness and price. It becomes its own option below, measured on its own.

3Ideas for your room

Every idea is standalone: it is measured on its own against your current room. Press Apply on as many as you like to build a plan. The running total appears above.
Prices are rough US retail estimates in USD. The model is simplified, so treat results as a guide.
